About nuteo solution

nuteo solution is a Bangkok-based engineering consultancy focused on backend systems and platform engineering. We work with engineering teams that need to ship reliable infrastructure — not PowerPoint decks about it.

What we believe

  • Operations is a product. Reliability, observability, and on-call health are user-facing features.
  • Done means deployed, monitored, and documented. Code that lives on a branch is half-done.
  • Senior engineers, not staff augmentation. Most engagements pair you with people who've been on call at 3am.
  • Plain language, working software, no magic. If we can't explain it on a whiteboard, we don't ship it.

How we work

Most engagements are 6–12 weeks, scoped to a concrete outcome (a migrated platform, a CI/CD overhaul, an incident review). We embed with your team, hand off everything we build, and stick around for the post-mortem that always follows.

Who we are

Founded in 2024 by engineers who've spent a decade building and operating production systems across logistics, fintech, and SaaS. Small team by design.

Where we work

Bangkok-based, working with teams across APAC and remote-first companies globally. Comfortable in English; Thai clients welcome.
